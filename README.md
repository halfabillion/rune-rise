# Rune Rise

A one-thumb stacking and fusing puzzle. Stack matching rune stones in crystal pillars, fuse five into the next tier, and keep the pillars from overflowing.

**Play:** https://halfabillion.github.io/rune-rise/

## Controls

Tap a pillar to pick it up, tap another to move. Fuse five matching stones to rise. Works with touch or mouse.
